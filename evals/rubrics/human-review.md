# Human comparison rubric

Compare outputs from the same brief with generator identity hidden and randomized
left/right order. Show matching viewports and the same supporting facts. Allow ties.

| Dimension | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Comprehension | Can you explain the audience, offer, commitment, and next step? |
| Credibility | Does the page stay within evidence and handle unknowns honestly? |
| Hierarchy | Can you locate the useful explanation and action without searching? |
| Brand and visual judgment | Does the design fit this particular offer and supplied constraints? |
| Interaction | Can you complete the intended journey and recover from mistakes? |

For each, record left/right/tie and a short reason. Aim for two independent
reviewers, retain disagreements, and report by brief. Aesthetic preference cannot
establish a conversion rate. Do not use an AI-authorship detector as a release gate.
