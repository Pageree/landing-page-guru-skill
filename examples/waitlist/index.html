A place for the unfinished

Good work starts with something you can't quite explain yet.

Bookmarks in one place. Notes in another. A thought you meant to come back to. Fieldwork explores a simpler way to keep the context around your ideas, from the first observation to the first draft.

01 / COLLECT

Bring the context along.

Keep a note beside the source that sparked it, so the idea still makes sense when you return.

02 / CONNECT

Follow an unexpected thread.

Group related observations without needing to know the final structure in advance.

03 / MAKE

Give your thinking a shape.

Move from a collection of fragments to a clear outline you can keep developing.
