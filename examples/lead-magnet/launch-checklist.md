# Before you send the link

An original sample resource from The Launch Notes, a fictional publication.

## The promise

- Can a visitor explain who the offer is for and what they get?
- Does the page match the promise in the ad, email, or referral?
- Can you trace each material claim to evidence and permission?
- Are price, commitment, and the next step clear where relevant?

## The journey

- Use the primary action on a narrow mobile screen and with a keyboard.
- Submit an empty form. Can you find and fix the errors?
- Submit valid synthetic details to an authorized test destination.
- Reject the request. Does the form preserve input and explain the next step?
- Disconnect the network after submitting. Does the page preserve the request,
  explain the uncertainty, and offer a safe next step?
- Retry safely. Does one logical request remain one lead?
- Distinguish backend acceptance from storage, notification, and receipt.
- Open the promised resource. Check the link, format, and actual contents.

## The evidence

- Count the business outcome, not every click or submission attempt.
- Reload the success page. It must not manufacture a new lead.
- Exercise configured refusal and withdrawal states for tracking.
- Keep personal data and secrets out of analytics and shared reports.
- Record what was checked, what failed, and what remains untested.
- Choose an evaluation method that fits your traffic and tools.

This is a practical inspection aid, not a conversion guarantee or legal certification.
